## 1. Anti-vision — `[MISSING]`

A Tuesday five years out, if this strategy quietly fails. Not a disaster, a normal working day. What
are you doing, who is in the room, what is the mood? Then: what exactly is rotten about that?

Be unkind and be specific. "A boring company" is not an answer.

## 2. Boundaries — `[MISSING]`

What you do not give up, whatever happens. A rate you will not go under, work you refuse, time you
will not trade, a market you will not leave, people you will not lose.

At least one of them should cost something. A boundary nobody would ever cross is not a boundary.
