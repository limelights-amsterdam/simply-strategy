This is what the reader carries into the meeting. Fill in only the tables that have content.

**The decision, in one paragraph.** Answer first. What we will do, why now, and what it costs. Six sentences at most.

**The choices:**

| We do | We do not | Why this side |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Construction firms above 50 staff only | Small business and consumer | Our quote takes 4 hours, which only pays above 50k order value |

**The assumptions this rests on:**

| Assumption | Must be true | How we test it | When we know |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Buyers search by part number | At least 40 percent of search traffic | Analyse 200 search terms | 15 September |

This is the most important table in the document. A strategy is a bet with a reason, and this is where the bet is written down. Put in the assumptions that could overturn the decision, not the safe ones.

**What it costs:**

| Item | Amount or effort | When |
| --- | --- | --- |

**The actions:**

| # | Action | Owner | Done by | Done means |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Analyse 200 search terms | Sanne | 15 Sep | Report with volume per term |

**What we are held to:**

| Measure | Now | Target | Date | Below this we stop |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

For a review, deliver a findings table instead:

| # | Where | Test | What is wrong | What it should say |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | p2, "leading partner" | Opposite test | Nobody would choose the reverse | Name the segment and the share |

Close a review with one line placing the document: is this a strategy, a plan, or a wish. That distinction helps more than a score.
