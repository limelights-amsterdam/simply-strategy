# Is there a decision in it?

The first guard says: if the material contains no decision, the page says so. This is how you tell.

## The one test that matters

A decision has a sacrifice. Search the material for stop, phase out, no longer, not, wind down,
decline, move away from, next to an activity, a product, a market or a customer. One real one is
enough. "We focus more sharply" and "we say no to what does not fit" name nothing and do not count.

Then look for what is picked up in its place. Stop plus start is a shift, and a shift is a row in
the From → To ledger.

## Four more, when the first passes

| Test | Question | If it fails, the gaps screen says |
|---|---|---|
| Number | Is there a quantity, a figure and a date? | `[TO FILL: the number that shows this worked, and by when]` |
| Owner | Who, by name or role, and by when? | `[TO FILL: owner]` |
| Mechanism | How does A lead to B? | The page keeps the claim and marks the missing middle step |
| Already true | What changes on Monday that was not true on Friday? | Nothing, but the page does not present a continuation as a choice |

## Placing the material

The gaps screen ends with one sentence that says what kind of document this is. Use the ladder, and
be honest about which rung:

| It is a | When | The sentence says |
|---|---|---|
| Strategy | A choice with a sacrifice and a number, actions with an owner and a date | The material decides, and says who does what by when |
| Strategy without execution | The choice is there, owners and dates are not | The material makes choices. It does not yet carry a plan, a budget or a name |
| Plan | Owners and dates, no choice underneath | The material schedules work. It does not choose between options |
| Direction | A sacrifice or a number, not both | The material points somewhere. It does not yet choose or commit |
| Wish | No sacrifice, no number, no owner | The material contains no decision. It describes a hope |

When the material is a wish, the page still shows the hero, the case if there is one, and the gaps.
The ledger and the numbers are left out, and the gaps screen says why. That page is short and that
is the correct result.

## What is not yours to do

You do not make the choice the material left open. You point at it. "The deck names security and
in-home as growth pillars but does not say which comes first" is the page's job. Picking one is not.
